# Bot research history loading

The advanced research loader queries the complete selected window from `assetSnapshots`, using `basis: 'xor-pool'`. Pair and XOR fee marks come from exact, same-state direct XOR pool reserves. It does not request USD prices or impose a stable-token peg. Indexed observations take precedence over older bundled observations, including historical corrections inside the selected window.

If indexed evidence has gaps, the bundled archive may supply those exact missing hours. A remaining gap of at most 24 hours may use the existing bounded historical RPC repair. Both fallbacks reconstruct direct-pool marks with the requested token metadata, chain genesis and current denomination. They cannot overwrite indexed observations. Invalid, duplicate, non-hourly, out-of-range or missing-fee observations do not enter the merge; unavailable sources leave actual gaps. No price is repeated or interpolated to manufacture coverage.

Every source is checked against the captured connection identity. Returned identity metadata must match it. Currency metadata is captured before asynchronous work and forms part of the cache key. The loader rechecks finalized identity after all sources and before returning a result. Genuine partial history remains available with an exact missing count; only complete windows enter the page-local 60-second cache. Incomplete windows retry the indexer on the next research attempt without reloading the page. A newly completed hourly boundary has a different key immediately. Cancelling/disposal invalidation prevents late responses from repopulating the cache.

Pure indexed history retains its chain/denomination identity without an archive label. Archive-only results retain `archive-pool-spot`; combined results use the existing `mixed-pool-spot-and-indexed` provenance kind. Both sides of this combined history now have the direct-pool basis. They remain historical spot marks, not executable historical quotes or fills.

Strategy Lab refreshes the end of new 30-day, 90-day and full-history batches to the latest completed hour. Saved/custom study dates stay fixed. GO requires its original 168 study closes plus 201 preceding hourly closes from the same indexed direct-pool source. The loader fetches all 369 hours together, retains boundary receipts and returns only the 168 study closes as funded history. Metadata readiness checks the same 369-hour coverage while preserving the original study and validation dates. The advanced fallback policy does not apply to GO.

The indexer's additive `assetHourlyCoverage` query reports structural per-asset proof and pool availability for up to 2,160 completed hours. It contains no prices. Its usable count is not pair qualification: consumers must still join input, output and XOR fee observations on identical boundary block proofs and validate current denomination and configured token precision. Research continues to retrieve and validate the actual `closeEvidence` records.

The indexed JSON decoder accepts bounded plain arrays as well as records. Live `closeEvidence` includes supplementary `pools` arrays, including empty arrays; these must not discard an otherwise valid direct-pool observation. Array entries remain subject to the existing size and depth bounds and cannot contain accessors, sparse slots or custom prototypes. Price and boundary validation still uses the required direct-pool proof fields.

Offline regression coverage is in `tests/unit/features/bot-trading/playground-history.spec.ts`, alongside the lower-level `history-pool.spec.ts` and `pool-history.spec.ts` decoder tests. These use synthetic fixtures and mocked sources, with no external services.

## GO signal context and saved evidence

New GO studies use `goal-episodes-v3`. Every episode receives the 201 closes immediately preceding its funding close, enough for the largest supported 202-observation signal including that close. Earlier marks warm signals only: they do not start funding, consume the capital budget, contribute performance, or carry portfolio state across episodes. The seven-day study still contains 117 training closes, one embargo close and 50 validation closes. It still evaluates four independently funded 24-hour training episodes and two validation episodes.

The assistant still receives exactly 117 training observations. Separate `signalWarmup` metadata discloses the preceding range and explicitly states that its prices are not supplied. Provider, Jev and desktop transports verify this range ends one hour before training; raw prefix prices and validation observations do not enter the drafting request. Episode workers receive only their own funded candles and preceding signal context, not the retained whole-study receipt.

The v3 history receipt binds input, output and fee-token identities, chain genesis, denomination, all 369 exact closes and their indexed closing/successor block receipts. Its SHA-256 commitment detects changed retained data; it is not independent chain attestation or a claim about exact historical browser arrival times. Immediately before review, the loader bypasses its brief cache and re-queries the same window. Indexed evidence pages use GraphQL network-only and HTTP no-store. Cancellation unsubscribes that reader so urql stops an unshared request without closing the shared client or cancelling another subscriber. Any changed observation, boundary, identity or indexed source invalidates the result. Existing v2 studies remain v2 and are never relabeled as warmed studies. Fees, order limits, capital, the protected fee reserve, and loss limits are unchanged.

Focused offline coverage includes `autopilot-history.spec.ts`, `autopilot.spec.ts`, `goal-history.spec.ts`, `goal-research.spec.ts`, `research-goal.spec.ts`, and the AI transport suites. Synthetic fixtures are not live trading evidence.

# Four-week external acquisition pilot

The campaign page was verified live on 25 September 2026. The execution targets below use actual dates; other release claims still need their own evidence. The audience is people who do not already follow SORA/Polkaswap: discovery-site visitors, TON wallet users, GateHub users, builders/LPs and independent editorial audiences. Existing Telegram/X channels serve reference and support; reposting there is not the acquisition experiment. No calendar invitations, posts or automations are created by this document. Roles below need a named owner in the [tracker](tracker.md). Paid-media budget: zero. Actual provider, inventory and subsidy commitments require a separately defined capped operating budget.

Use the [eight verified destinations](external-acquisition.md) and [tailored submissions](external-outreach.md). E01–E03 are the first batch; E04–E08 are independent next actions. Do not spend week 1 rewriting generic social posts while these prepared submissions wait.

## Expansion and stop decisions

Expand a working external destination only after attribution, mobile flow and recovery pass. If 100 measured external visits produce no meaningful previews or newcomer interest, investigate audience/message fit before pursuing a larger placement. If people preview but cannot fund, prioritize that specific funding barrier; do not interpret the drop as a need for more reach. A payment quote for XOR cannot guarantee a later TS allocation as the campaign cap moves. Stop new provider orders when supportable delivery terms cannot be established; this does not gate the existing direct burn. Pause promotional expansion for ownership/allocation mismatches, repeated route failures or wallet-recovery defects. Pause paid acquisition whenever its attributable contribution cannot cover its cost. Keep the current direct-burn availability policy intact.

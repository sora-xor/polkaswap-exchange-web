## Long announcement

### Explore Tonswap. Understand the XOR → TS campaign.

Tonswap's public testnet lets you explore trading, liquidity and launchpad workflows on TON. Start with test assets and see the product for yourself: https://tonswap.org/

Separately, the XOR burn campaign on Polkaswap lets eligible participants reserve a future TS allocation. Your signing SORA wallet owns that future claim. Claims are intended to open on Tonswap at launch; the burn does not deliver spendable TS today.

Here is how to participate:

1. Open https://polkaswap.io/#/burn?campaign=tonswap and select the TONSWAP campaign.
2. Connect a SORA wallet you control, with native XOR available for the amount and transaction fee.
3. Review the estimated allocation, campaign rules and irreversible-burn notice.
4. If you decide to proceed, sign with that wallet and follow the receipt through finality and indexing.
5. Keep control of the signing wallet and save the transaction record for the future claim.

The reward rate falls as eligible burns accumulate, from a marginal 50 TS per XOR at the start of the curve to 5 at its endpoint. Each allocation integrates the curve across that burn. Transactions ahead of yours can change the estimate.

The rewarded cap is 1,753,357 XOR. The full cap allocates 48,217,317.5 TS. Any excess XOR is still burned and earns no TS; the SORA Trust account is excluded from rewards.

New to SORA? First confirm a supported wallet and a route that delivers XOR on the SORA network. A token with the same ticker on another network is not enough. Keep a recovery backup, and never share it with support.

Read the campaign terms: https://tonswap.org/ts

Preview your allocation: https://polkaswap.io/#/burn?campaign=tonswap

## Guided-onboarding release post

Interested in the TS campaign but don't hold XOR?

The guided flow on Polkaswap helps you choose a supported wallet and funding route, return to the TONSWAP campaign, and review your XOR burn before signing. You stay in control of the SORA wallet that owns the future TS claim.

Check the current route availability and total costs, including the burn fee. The allocation estimate can change before your transaction finalizes. XOR burns are irreversible, and amounts beyond the campaign cap earn no TS.

## Working rules

The canonical campaign destination is [tonswap.org/ts](https://tonswap.org/ts); the deployment workstream confirmed it live and verified on 25 September 2026. The prepared participation deep link is [the TONSWAP campaign](https://polkaswap.io/#/burn?campaign=tonswap); consult the tracker for the separate Polkaswap release evidence. The existing [Polkaswap burn page](https://polkaswap.io/#/burn) remains available.

The [campaign specification](../../tonswap-burn.md) is the implementation reference: the SORA signer owns a future TS claim; exact marked finalized burns determine allocations; the reward cap is shared; excess XOR can still burn without a reward. Preserve [existing burn availability](../../tonswap-burn-availability-2026-09-21.md).

The [official Tonswap homepage](https://tonswap.org/) identifies the public testnet and future mainnet. Label testnet footage. Do not present a testnet balance as a spendable mainnet TS allocation.

The immediate cash mechanism is still unspecified. The campaign owner needs to name the receiving entity, revenue source, amount/deadline and costs. Burning XOR does not itself send money to that entity. Keep paid acquisition at zero until there is a defined, measurable contribution model.

## Release handling

The user redirected execution to the guided purchase and burn flow. External outreach is paused and existing drafts remain unsent. Prioritize the Get TS funding implementation and verification. Existing official channels provide a stable reference and support destination; repeating posts to those same followers does not solve the stated awareness problem. The guided-onboarding copy is explicitly conditional on a verified production release. Provider candidates remain research leads until the network, denomination, current quote and delivery path are proven. Prepared outreach is not a claim that a provider agreed to partner.

Do not wait for every proposed integration before improving the existing route. Keep gaps visible in the tracker, and attach actual evidence when marking work complete.

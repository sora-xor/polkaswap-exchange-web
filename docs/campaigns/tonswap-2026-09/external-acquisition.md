# External audience acquisition queue

Verified against public primary sources on 25 September 2026. Eight independent destinations are qualified below. This replaces an acquisition strategy based on repeatedly posting to existing SORA/Polkaswap Telegram and X followers. Those channels remain useful for reference and support, but their traffic is not evidence of a new audience.

No outreach, form submission, account creation, payment or placement has been executed by this research workstream. A published contact route establishes reachability, not acceptance, free placement, endorsement or audience size. Use the [individual drafts](external-outreach.md); record actual actions in the [tracker](tracker.md).

## First 72 hours

1. Verify the deployed [campaign page](https://tonswap.org/ts), [participation link](https://polkaswap.io/#/burn?campaign=tonswap), mobile journey and dated terms. Make a product demo using clearly labeled TON testnet assets. A demo invitation can proceed while video recording is outstanding; do not claim an attachment exists.
2. Submit the normal TON App product listing through an existing Telegram login, explicitly identifying the public testnet. Send the tailored My Wallet and GateHub invitations to their published business contacts. These are the three closest audience/use-case matches.
3. Prepare Product Hunt's product draft through an existing personal account. Publish only with a working immediate testnet experience and the correct beta/mainnet status. A token-reservation page alone is not the product.
4. Send the STON.fi education proposal and CryptoSlate editorial inquiry. Hand the Defiant draft to a human sender because its terms explicitly exclude automated submissions. Offer Decrypt a separate explanatory interview, not a paid press-release package.
5. After five business days, follow up once where appropriate with new useful evidence, such as a working demo or a corrected newcomer flow. Do not repeatedly message a silent recipient. Log rejection reasons and move effort toward destinations that engage.

## Routes deliberately excluded from immediate execution

- The old [TON Hubs social-promotion opportunity](https://builders.ton.org/opportunities/hubs-socials) explicitly excludes token-related news and testnet-only announcements. Its historical eligibility also requires traction and other conditions. The [old founder resource portal](https://builders.ton.org/founder/resources) now redirects to the TON homepage. Do not present either as an open, eligible burn-promotion submission.
- [ton-community/awesome-ton](https://github.com/ton-community/awesome-ton) is archived/read-only (3 September 2025); a new listing PR there is not an actionable route.
- [TON Infra's deployer directory](https://toninfra.com/deployers) identifies mainnet projects. Tonswap's public testnet is not a verified match for a mainnet listing.
- Bemo and Tonstakers have relevant LP audiences, but this research did not verify a current partnership/editorial contact distinct from support or privacy. They are follow-on research leads, not padded entries in the qualified queue.
- Reddit/Telegram community posting without a verified relevant posting policy or moderator path is not in the initial send queue. No group-member scraping, unsolicited member DMs, mass mentions or bought followers.

## Referral labels and outcome reporting

Reserve these non-personal labels, one per organization: `ton_app`, `my_wallet`, `gatehub`, `product_hunt`, `ston_dev`, `cryptoslate`, `the_defiant`, `decrypt`. A proposed destination has the form `https://tonswap.org/ts?ref=ton_app`. Use only fixed allowlisted values; never append a recipient email, wallet or individual identifier.

These labels are a campaign naming contract, **not evidence of implemented attribution**. The current `tonswap_funnel.source` enum describes product locations and must remain `campaign|swap|deposit|bridge`; it cannot be repurposed as a partner ID. Until ref handling and actual aggregate collection are verified, publish the plain canonical link and use partner-reported aggregate clicks plus voluntary study answers. See [measurement specification](analytics-events.md).

For every destination record: date/action, sender, copy version, result, public placement URL, reported aggregate exposure/clicks with the source's definition, newcomer interviews, supported journey completions, and support cost. Count a message as sent only with delivery/submission evidence; count a placement only with a live URL. Ask interview participants whether they already followed SORA/Polkaswap before the placement; do not silently classify all external traffic as first-time awareness.

The causal test is: **new audience discovers the product → understands the TS claim → can reach a supported funding route → independently chooses whether to participate**. Testnet testers are not counted as TS purchasers. Burn volume is not cash revenue, and distinct SORA addresses are not distinct people.

# Polkaswap tea shipping — reviewed 27 September 2026

This is Polkaswap Community Store fulfillment policy, maintained with its merchant configuration. It is not part of the reusable Sora Pay package. See the [Store overview](../community-store.md).

The configured store accepts orders for **203 countries and territories**, including Japan. This matrix is specific to sealed 100 g Shizuoka organic sencha, pure processed *Camellia sinensis*, sold to individuals for consumption. It is not a claim that every product, quantity or postcode is accepted. Japan Post's actual service status is checked daily; volunteers confirm the address and complete the documented import requirements before dispatch. No customer personal-use checkbox is collected.

The tea remains **1.759225 XOR**. Shipping is also frozen in XOR until an explicit catalog revision. Daily exchange rates, XOR market prices and new postal tariff prices never reprice published orders. Internal launch conversion is separate from customs valuation: declare the actual paid sale truthfully under the destination's valuation rules, not a false gift or arbitrary token-market value.

## Fulfill an order

1. Claim the order in the private queue. Buy sealed stock; check producer, ingredients, Japan origin, net weight, lot and best-before. Keep its organic label accurate. The packaging estimates are 120 g per bag plus 80 g per outer parcel; weigh the actual parcel and check postal dimensions before dispatch.
2. Use the destination's configured service below and its exact country conditions. Complete EAD, CN22/CN23 and invoice requirements. Declare a retail merchandise sale: Japanese dried green tea, *Camellia sinensis*, origin Japan. HS090210 covers green tea in immediate packs of 3 kg or less; complete the destination tariff suffix where required.
3. Finish every applicable permit, food clearance, phytosanitary certificate, organic certificate and importer requirement before mailing. Conditional destinations remain available because this paperwork can be handled during fulfillment. A customer's private-use intent does not itself exempt a seller's retail shipment from the rules.
4. Honor the quoted XOR total. Some costs, including US prepaid duties/app fees and permits, are not included in the Japan Post postage-only tariff. Volunteers must cover such sender charges or arrange a lawful disclosed solution; never silently charge the wallet again. If the order cannot be fulfilled, refund under its saved policy.
5. Record dispatch and give the customer tracking where provided. Small packets labelled **untracked** do not acquire tracking merely because EAD is submitted. Surface routes may take substantially longer; use the actual carrier estimate. Domestic Letter Pack Plus is tracked but has no loss compensation.

Current refund policy returns the XOR received for tea and shipping, less the actual outbound SORA refund fee. The customer's original transfer fee is separate. Historical orders retain their saved policy and any explicitly agreed amendment. Keep funds for outstanding obligations.

## Destination requirements that differ materially

- **US:** file FDA prior notice before mailing. Japan Post resumed retail merchandise on 14 April 2026 through its designated-office, recommended-app prepaid-duty workflow; use the linked DDP label, not an unrelated MyPage label. Verify the destination-specific process for US territories and the freely associated Pacific states. Qualifying online personal purchases have the documented USDA organic certificate exemption; APHIS admits fully manufactured dried tea. Sources: [Japan Post](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/send/oversea/information/2026/0413_01.html), [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/importing-food-products-united-states/prior-notice-imported-foods), [APHIS](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/ace/generally-authorized-non-propagative-plant-products), [USDA](https://www.ams.usda.gov/rules-regulations/strengthening-organic-enforcement/faq).
- **EU and Britain:** complete the applicable organic certificate/importer route before dispatch. JAS equivalence alone is not a consignment certificate. The review did not establish a blanket direct-to-consumer postal exemption. Northern Ireland follows EU rules. [EU organic trade](https://agriculture.ec.europa.eu/farming/organic-farming/trade_en), [GB organic imports](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/importing-organic-food-to-the-uk).
- **France and associated destinations:** food is prohibited in EMS for FR/AD/MC/GP/BL/MF/GF/MQ/RE/YT/PM. The catalog uses a permitted parcel route instead. **India** likewise excludes tea from EMS and uses an ordinary parcel with the required phytosanitary/FSSAI paperwork.
- **Country caps:** Argentina's simplified route permits three identical units; Thailand tea 1 kg; Singapore processed food 5 kg and S$100. The Singapore catalog has eight bags, using the recorded MUFG SGD/JPY snapshot (124.09 on 25 September); recheck the actual customs value before dispatch. Taiwan retains its established 6 kg gross band/49-bag boundary. Latvia/Lithuania use the published 200 g personal-tea route; larger consignments require clarified ordinary clearance. These are route/packaging limits, not stock limits.
- **Japan:** Letter Pack Plus costs ¥600 per designated envelope nationwide, with a 4 kg physical limit. The packing model uses two sealed bags per envelope (estimated 320 g including envelope/packing allowance); larger orders are split and priced by the number of envelopes. The checkout weight is an aggregate quantity boundary, not a claim that a multi-envelope shipment fits in one envelope. Verify the two-bag fit without cutting or altering the designated envelope. [Japan Post Letter Pack](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/letterpack/index_en.html).

## Maintain the catalog

The source records are [tea destination records](../../tools/community-store/shipping/tea-destinations.json), [postal rate snapshot](../../tools/community-store/shipping/japan-post-rates.json) and [postal availability snapshot](../../tools/community-store/shipping/japan-post-availability.json). They retain source URLs, reviewed limits and the source snapshot; they are historical evidence, not a permanently fresh availability fallback. Rates originate from [Japan Post's official tariff schedule](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/send/oversea/charge/index_en.html). Daily availability comes from the [official service chart](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/send/oversea/information/overview_en.html).

Build with Node26/Yarn, then use `node tools/community-store/build-catalog.mjs --base <merchant.json> --output <new-merchant.json>` to regenerate the reviewed catalog offline. The output must be a new file; the base merchant's enabled flag, product price, internal frozen conversion, recipient, chain, refund policy, origin allowlist and operator settings remain unchanged. Review destination research before changing the structured records, and regenerate unit/boundary checks. The supplied worldwide template is disabled.

For the current deployment, use the approved MOF runtime-only update path, pause new admissions while preserving order recovery, keep the database/cursor and secrets intact, verify the exact runtime/merchant manifest, and complete manual plus scheduled encrypted backup restores before reopening. This catalog/provider update uses the unchanged 0.2.2 browser contract and requires no IPFS origin change or Bunny purge. A later frontend deployment must still follow the normal full IPFS/Bunny runbook.

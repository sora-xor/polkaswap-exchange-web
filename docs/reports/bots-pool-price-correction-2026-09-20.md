# Direct-pool history correction

20 September 2026. This fixes a real source mismatch behind the Bots research results. It does not establish a profitable strategy or a finalized trade.

## Root cause and measured effect

GO divided the indexer's global USD closes to derive a pair price. The indexer fixes KUSD at USD 1 and selects a liquidity-based discovery route for XOR. In the 117 observations actually supplied to the prior Chrome attempt, XOR's USD route used DAI in 109 hours and KUSD→PSWAP→XOR in eight. Two large jumps coincided with route changes. All 117 supplied closes matched this USD calculation; none of the retained winning routes was the direct KUSD/XOR pool.

The repeated values were not interpolated missing hours: all close block hashes were distinct, and each USD close reconstructed from its retained route reserves. The bug was using these global USD marks as the pair's comparable history. The frontend also ignored the existing route evidence.

A new backfill of actual direct reserves recovers the original 117 training hours. Their USD-derived marks understated direct KUSD/XOR marks by 5.43%–43.07%. The unchanged 10 KUSD plus 1 XOR idle-allocation diagnostic exceeds 5% peak drawdown in **two of four** days using direct marks, versus four using the old series. The earlier every-day result and any impossibility arguments depending on those old prices therefore do not describe the real pair. This comparison uses training observations only; it does not fit a replacement strategy or inspect held-out returns.

## Correction

- Indexer commit `f8de51da62fed4eebfafb9d218f1c13708636b67` retains `closeEvidence.xorPool` independently of USD pricing. Exact reserves and observed precision come from the same finalized state. Legacy incomplete evidence stays absent; confirmed absence is null. Duplicate or ambiguous direct pools are rejected. No missing precision is assumed to be 18.
- Backfill preparation retains all relevant direct XOR pools and their metadata, even when another route wins USD discovery. Same-block repair upgrades existing records without resetting the indexer's checkpoint or inventing observations.
- The frontend derives pair and fee marks from exact integer reserve ratios, dividing once after composing the two sides. It checks asset identity, precision, denomination, matching block and successor evidence, and completed-hour timestamps. Missing/legacy/ambiguous rows remain gaps; USD pegs are never a fallback for this flow.
- `goal-episodes-v2` uses this source for both GO research and episode-qualified live signals. V1 qualification snapshots require a new study. Budgets, fees, loss thresholds, partial-order constraints and wallet authorization remain unchanged.

The archive produced 192 hours / 1,344 major-token records covering September 12 06:00 through September 20 06:00 UTC. Its SHA-256 is `e67333b7b72fc0e06794e76278ba2f6cb746e0562efd3747d63d5a8635fb99c6`. The actual frontend decoder verifies 192/192 observations for KUSD/XOR, VAL/XOR, PSWAP/XOR, DAI/XOR and LLD/XOR.

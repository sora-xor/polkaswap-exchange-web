At 16:43 JST the page's fresh finalized network-fee observation, valued at the last completed hourly KUSD/XOR pool mark, implied that one network fee would consume about 90.7% of the goal's 5% loss allowance. This is a dated explanatory scenario, not an executable quote or a proof that every later opportunity will fail. A two-leg round trip pays the fixed network fee twice, before pool fee, impact, and slippage. Shrinking an order does not shrink the fixed network fee. The current account also held only about 9.10 KUSD and 0.12 XOR when queried read-only, below the goal's 10 KUSD input budget plus 1 XOR reserve.

Research supports *abstention* when measured edge cannot clear costs. A [2026 hourly BTC walk-forward study](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00060v1) found that cost-aware filtering reduced turnover, but its selected strategies did not significantly beat buy-and-hold after its corrected comparisons. Its market, liquidity and fee scale differ from SORA, so it supplies a testing method rather than an XOR return estimate. [No-trade-region analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07949) gives a theoretical reason to avoid marginal trades under costs, but its simplified assumptions are not a live SORA strategy. [Research on LLM-generated trading rules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27734) reinforces trial logging and leakage-safe, future validation. [BIS's AMM analysis](https://www.bis.org/publications/trading-defi-era-automated-market-maker) explains why pool fees, curve impact and ordering matter in addition to network fees. The [DEX arbitrage study revised 2026-09-09](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08302) adds fee competition, ordering and inventory risk; the local route audit found no second viable venue to assume an arbitrage fill.

A testable next hypothesis is a sparse, partial-inventory rule with an explicit no-trade band: buy XOR only when a frozen forecast margin exceeds the same-state exact quote's fee, pool cost, impact, slippage and uncertainty allowance. Keep unspent KUSD and the separate XOR fee reserve in valuation. Compare final XOR value against both unchanged KUSD inventory and an immediate XOR buy-and-hold baseline. Predeclare candidate count and evaluate future, nonoverlapping 24-hour windows; do not recycle exposed validation data. Hourly spot marks can support signals and valuation, but cannot establish sub-hour executable fills or a predictive edge on their own. A model draft remains a hypothesis until these tests qualify it, and trading still needs the user's wallet approval.

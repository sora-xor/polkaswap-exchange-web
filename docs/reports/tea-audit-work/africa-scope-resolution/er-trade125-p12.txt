any trading licence of such dealer until a court
verdict is rendered on the subject matter.

(e) Where the stocks of the declared articles have
been disposed of pursuant to the provisions of
paragraph (d) of this Article, the Minister shall
order that payment be made to the dealer of the
proceeds realized on their sale less any
expenses incurred in connection with their
seizure and sale.

Alphabetical List, Tristan da Cunha

TRISTAN DA CUNHA

 REVISED EDITION OF THE LAWS, 2017

 ALPHABETICAL LIST OF ORDINANCES WITH TEXT

  • This is a list in alphabetical order of all Tristan da Cunha Ordinances, the text of which is available by clicking the links.
  • Part A lists Ordinances enacted by the Tristan da Cunha legislature; Part B lists St Helena Ordinances that apply to Tristan da Cunha and that have been modified by the Law Revision Commissioner under the Revised Edition of the Laws Ordinance, 1999. See the section on ‘Laws of St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha’ in the General Introduction to the website.
  • Tristan da Cunha subsidiary legislation is listed in the Categories List and can be found after the text of the respective Ordinance. St Helena subsidiary legislation that applies to Tristan da Cunha is listed in the List of SH laws applied to Tristan da Cunha.
  • For UK laws that apply to Tristan da Cunha, see the List of UK laws that apply to St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha.

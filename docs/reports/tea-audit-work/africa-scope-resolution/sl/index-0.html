🔥 A fire destroyed hundreds of thousands of African gazettes. We're asking librarians and funders to help preserve Gazettes before more are lost.

How you can help

We welcome donations of gazette collections. We will scan and digitise gazettes for free. Please get in touch to donate gazettes.

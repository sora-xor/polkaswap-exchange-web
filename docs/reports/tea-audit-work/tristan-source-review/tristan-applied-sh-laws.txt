                      REVISED EDITION OF THE LAWS, 2017

                ST HELENA LAWS APPLIED TO TRISTAN DA CUNHA

    All St Helena Laws extant at 1 January 2018 (including UK laws applied to St Helena)
     apply to Tristan da Cunha with exceptions and modifications by virtue of the
     Application of St Helena Law (Tristan da Cunha) Ordinance, 1987 (ASHL(TC)O
     1987).
    The following is a list by category of St Helena laws that apply to Tristan da Cunha,
     either by their own terms, by virtue of the ASHL(TC)O, 1987 or by another Ordinance
     enacted in Tristan da Cunha
    St Helena Ordinances that have an equivalent in Tristan da Cunha do not apply –
     section 3(c) of the ASHL(TC)O, 1987. Some St Helena Ordinances are not appropriate
     for application or are expressed to be limited to St Helena.
    The precise way in which other St Helena laws apply to Tristan da Cunha, if not
     expressly stated in a TdaC Ordinance, needs to be judged by reference to section 2(2)
     of the ASHL(TC)O, 1987. This list is therefore not to be taken as authoritative. For
     guidance, see the court cases at the General Introduction to the website
    The text of the St Helena laws can be accessed on the St Helena section of the website.

What is HS Code?
Harmonized System that is used to classify
goods and determine customs procedures &
duty rates. Previously, items consisted of 8-
Digit HS codes and are updated by adding 4
numbers to convert to 12-Digit HS codes.

Integrated Customs Tariff
Includes:
o Customs HS codes
o Customs duty rate
o Free Trade Agreements
o Anti-Dumping rate
o Value Added Tax & Excise Tax
o Local & Federal Restriction and Prohibition
  Controlling Authorities
Integrated Customs Tariff
    Reasons for Transitions
GCC has published the Tariff Integrated Schedule based
on Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding
System of the WCO (2022), updating the GCC HS Codes
from 8-digits to 12-digits


Based on Cabinet Resolution No. (119) of 2024 in regards
to approval of the integrated customs tariff
nomenclature based on Harmonized Commodity
Description and Coding System of the GCC states.


Staring August 2025, a new 12-digit harmonized system
will be implemented to enhance accuracy, reduce
potential discrepancies, and promote standardization of
customs procedures.
               Benefits of Transition to GCC HS Code
While challenges such as misidentification, classification errors, and increased complexity are anticipated, the
benefits of alignment with the GCC mandate make this transition essential.

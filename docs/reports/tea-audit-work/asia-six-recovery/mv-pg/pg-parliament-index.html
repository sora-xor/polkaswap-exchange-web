Home ❯ Parliamentary Business ❯ Bills and Legislation

Explore the legislative heartbeat of Papua New Guinea. Access a comprehensive library of bills, from those recently passed into law to those currently under parliamentary debate. Utilize our search and filter tools to find specific documents and understand their impact.Gain deeper insights into the legislative process by tracking the progress of each bill through various stages of review and approval.

The National Parliament of Papua New Guinea is the legislative body of the country, consisting of 118 members elected from single-member electorates.

Papua New Guinea Biosecurity Authority

About PNGBA

The Papua New Guinea Biosecurity Authority (PNGBA) formerly known as the National Agriculture & Quarantine Inspection Authority (NAQIA) is a government agency responsible for regulating the import and export of agricultural products in Papua New Guinea. The NAQIA was established in 1994, following the adoption of the Quarantine Act 1994.

The PNGBA plays a number of key roles in the agriculture sector, including:

  1. Administering the Quarantine Act 1994 and other relevant legislation
  2. Inspecting and testing agricultural products to ensure that they meet quarantine and health standards
  3. Regulating the import and export of agricultural products to and from Papua New Guinea
  4. Providing technical advice and assistance to the government and industry stakeholders
  5. Collaborating with other agencies and organizations to support the sustainable development of the agriculture sector in Papua New Guinea.

The PNGBA works to ensure that the import and export of agricultural products in Papua New Guinea is carried out in a safe and responsible manner, to protect the health of humans, animals, and plants. The PNGBA also plays a key role in supporting the development of the agriculture sector in Papua New Guinea.

Policy & Regulation

The Quarantine Act 1994

This act establishes the National Agriculture & Quarantine Inspection Authority (NAQIA) and sets out the powers and responsibilities of the agency. It also sets out the rules and regulations related to the import and export of agricultural products in Papua New Guinea.

The Plant Protection Act 1997

This act establishes the Plant Protection Committee and sets out the rules and regulations related to the import and export of plant products in Papua New Guinea. It also provides for the inspection, testing, and certification of plant products to ensure that they meet quarantine and health standards.

The Animal Health and Production Act 2002

This act establishes the Animal Health and Production Committee and sets out the rules and regulations related to the import and export of animal products in Papua New Guinea. It also provides for the inspection, testing, and certification of animal products to ensure that they meet quarantine and health standards.

The Food Safety Act 2013

This act establishes the Food Safety Authority of Papua New Guinea and sets out the rules and regulations related to the safety and quality of food products in Papua New Guinea. It also provides for the inspection, testing, and certification of food products to ensure that they meet health and safety standards.

The Trade and Industry Development Act 2015

This act establishes the Trade and Industry Development Authority of Papua New Guinea and sets out the rules and regulations related to the trade and industry sectors in Papua New Guinea. It also provides for the regulation of the import and export of goods and services in the country.

When & How to Contact PNGBA

The Papua New Guinea Biosecurity Authority (PNGBA) formerly known as the National Agriculture & Quarantine Inspection Authority (NAQIA) is a trade facilitation agency in Papua New Guinea that provides information and assistance related to quarantine and health standards, import and export regulations, and other issues related to the agriculture sector.

They provide support for farmers, businesses, government and industry stakeholders, and members of the public who have questions or concerns related to the import or export of agricultural products.

They can be contacted by visiting their website, emailing them at info@naqia.gov.pg, or calling them at +675 303 5888.

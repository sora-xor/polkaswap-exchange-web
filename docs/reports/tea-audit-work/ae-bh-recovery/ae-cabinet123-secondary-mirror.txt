                   Cabinet Resolution No. (123) of 2024
On the Approval of the Lists of Prohibited and Restricted Goods in the States
                                  of the GCC

The Cabinet:
− Upon reviewing the Constitution;
− Federal Decree-Law No. (14) of 2021 Establishing the Federal Authority for Identity,
  Citizenship, Customs and Ports Security (ICP);
− Federal Decree No. (15) of 2022 Ratifying the Unified Customs Law for the GCC States
  and its Executive Regulation;
− Cabinet Resolution No. (17) of 2022 Approving the Unified Commodity Description and
  Coding Table of the GCC States according to the 2022 Harmonized System and updates
  thereof;
− Based on the presentation of the Chairman of the Federal Authority for Identity,
   Citizenship, Customs and Ports Security (ICP), and the approval of the Cabinet,
Has resolved:

                                                     Article (1)
Lists of Prohibited and Restricted Goods in the States of the GCC approved by Financial and
Economic Cooperation Committee of the Gulf Cooperation Council attached to this
Resolution shall be approved.

                                                     Article (2)
The competent entities in the State shall adhere to the lists of prohibited and restricted
goods, each within its jurisdiction. The Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs,
and Port Security shall be responsible for ensuring the enforcement of these lists of
prohibited and restricted goods across the State.

                                                     Article (3)
Any provision that violates or contradicts the provisions of this Resolution shall be repealed.

                                                     Article (4)
This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall enter into force as of 01
January 2025.




Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum
Prime Minister

On: 02 Jumada II, 1446 A.H.
Corresponding to: 04 November 2024 AD

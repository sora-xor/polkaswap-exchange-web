REGULATIONS FOR THE IMPORTATION AND TREATMENT OF PLANT
AND PLANT PRODUCTS

    1. The importation of all planting materials requires a permit from the Ministry of Agriculture,
         Plant Protection and Quarantine Unit. Whenever necessary, treatment would be at the
         importers expense. Permits require at least two (2) weeks advance notice before the time
         you intend to import the planting materials.
    2. Confiscated materials must be destroyed by incineration. All products retained for
         inspectors’ examination and not claimed within two days will be destroyed without liability
         to the Ministry of Agriculture.

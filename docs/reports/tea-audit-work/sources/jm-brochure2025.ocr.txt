ROLE & FUNCTION

The basic purpose of the Plant
Quarantine /Produce Inspection
(PQ/PI) Branch is to:

1. Protect Jamaica from the
introduction of harmful plant
pests and diseases.

2. Ensure that only the highest
quality, pest free produce is
imported into and exported
from Jamaica.

LEGAL FRAMEWORK

The laws governing the activities
of the PQ/PI Branch are:

1. The Plant Quarantine Act,
1993 and it’s regulations

2. The Agriculture Produce Act,
1926 and it’s regulations

ICK TIPS

Two major documents required for
the importation of produce into
Jamaica are;

1. An import permit.

2. A phytosanitary certificate.

What is an Import Permit
(IP)?

It is a document issued by PQ/PI
Branch authorizing the importation of
agricultural commodities. It further

outlines the conditions that are
necessary for importation and should
therefore be sent to the supplier before
ordering, for use by the inspectorate of
the exporting country, prior to
shipping.

What is a Phytosanitary
Certificate?

This is a document issued by the
competent authority of the exporting
country. It certifies that the shipment
has been inspected and found to be
free of harmful pests and diseases. It
further certifies any special condition
required by the importing country as
stated on the Import Permit.

What category of produce
requires an IP?

An Import Permit is required for the
importation of ALL fresh fruits and
vegetables, plant parts and seeds.

What are some agricultural

items that do not require an IP?

1. Frozen/canned/dried fruits and
vegetables

2. Shredded vegetables

3. Processed material in general.

Are there any restrictions on
what can be imported?

Yes. If the importation of the
commodity poses a threat to Jamaica’s
agriculture, then that item will not be
allowed. A Pest Risk Analysis (PRA) is
conducted to access the phytosanitary
risk associated with the commodity.

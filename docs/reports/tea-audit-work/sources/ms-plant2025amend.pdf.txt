THE PLANT PROTECTION (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULES) REGULATIONS
2025 MADE BY THE GOVERNOR UNDER SECTION 15 OF THE PLANT
PROTECTION ACT (CAP. 9.02).


1.   Citation
         These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection
         (Amendment of Schedules) Regulations, 2025.

2.   First Schedule amended
         The First Schedule of the Plant Protection Regulations (Cap. 9.02)
         is amended—
                (a) in column 1 by deleting paragraph (b) and substituting
                    “All plants of the citrus family and parts of such plants
                    except the fruits”;
                (b) in column 2 paragraph (b) by inserting “Barbados” after
                    “Jamaica,”; and
                (c) by deleting paragraph (r) of column 1 and of column 2.

3.   Second Schedule amended
         The Second Schedule of the Plant Protection Regulations (Cap.
         9.02) is amended—
                (a) in column 1 by deleting paragraph (e) and substituting
                    “All plants of the citrus family and parts of such plants
                    except the fruits”;
                (b) in column 3 paragraph (e) by inserting “Barbados” after
                    “Jamaica,”; and

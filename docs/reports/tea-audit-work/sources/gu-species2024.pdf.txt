Compiled List of Acceptable and Banned Species for Entry into Guam – Updated June 2022

Mammals:

    •    Accepted
            o Dogs
            o Cats
            o Mice for research or for projects to control brown tree snake populations ONLY
            o Pet rabbits that have been spayed or neutered
    •    Banned
            o Mice and rodents for breeding or pet sale
            o Rabbit species for game, breeding, or sale
            o Unaltered/intact pet rabbits
            o Guinea pigs
            o Chinchilla
            o Ferrets
            o Hedgehogs
            o Sugar gliders
            o Other Canidae species including hybrids

Birds:

    •    Accepted
            o Poultry from the United States
            o Lady Gouldian finches
            o Zebra finches
            o European society finches
            o Red rumped parrots (parakeets)
            o Lovebirds
            o Diamond doves
            o Green cheeked conures
            o Caique
            o Cockatiels
            o Macaws
                   ▪ Blue and gold macaw
                   ▪ Scarlet macaw
                   ▪ Hyacinth macaw
                   ▪ Green winged macaw
            o African Grey parrot
            o Sun Conure
            o Common parakeet/budgerigar
            o Ostriches allowable on agriculture land with appropriate housing and care plan (site
                approval by Territory Vet and DAWR required)
            o Peking ducks (in theory but only allowable from the US and Hawaii won’t allow transit)
            o Domestic turkeys (on agricultural land only)
                          Animal Health and Control

    •   Banned
           o Poultry from any other region other than the United States
           o Pigeons
                  ▪ Rock doves
                  ▪ Homing pigeons
                  ▪ King pigeons
           o Monk/quaker parakeets
           o Indian ring neck parakeet (aka rose-ringed parakeet)
           o All species of quails
           o All species of pheasants
                  ▪ Including ring-neck pheasants
           o Java finches
           o Eclectus parrots
           o Lace neck dove
           o Cockatoos
           o Alexadrine parrot
           o Violet Indian ringneck
           o Yellow-fronted canary
           o Green singing finch
           o Ring necked doves
           o Guinea fowl
           o Quail
           o Peacocks

Reptiles & Amphibians – none allowed

Fish and Aquatic species are issued or denied permits directly through DAWR

Insects and Invertebrate species are issued or denied permits directly through Biosecurity

Packing Tips
Any restricted article at the time of importation or offer for importation to Guam shall not be packed in a pack-
ing material unless the plants were packed in the packing material immediately prior to shipment; such packing
material must be free from sand, soil, or earth and has not been used previously as packing material. For the list
of packing materials, visit https://www.govregs.com/regulations/7/319.37-9.

and specific restrictions for importation. Otherwise, the plants will be refused entry and will be sent back to its
country of origin. These restrictions are imposed to prevent the introduction of pests and diseases into the United
States, including Guam.

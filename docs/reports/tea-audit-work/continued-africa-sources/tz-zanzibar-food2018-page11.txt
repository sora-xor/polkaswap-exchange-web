2.4.4

A registered Food Importer shall be required to apply for Import permit
by fill in prescribed forms (Annex 3 for registrable foods and Annex 4 for
non-registrable foods).

Application for Import permit shall be accompanied with the following
documents;

a) Proforma invoice/Invoice from the supplier
b) Certificate of Origin

c) Bill of Lading

d) Packing List

e) Phytosanitary Certificate

f) Health Certificate

g) Fumigation Certificate

h) Certificate of Conformance

And where applicable;

a) Certificate of analysis

b) Radiation free certificate

c) Document show Minimum Residue Limit (MRL) of antibiotics or
pesticide

d) Halal Certificate

Application for importation of non-registrable products shall be
accompanied with pre-shipment samples for analysis whose cost will be
incurred by importer as prescribed in the Fees and Charges ZFDA Fees
and Charges Document. Proforma invoices shall not contain non-food
products, while food products and food supplements shall be in separate
proforma invoices.

Proforma invoice shall indicate the following for each food to be imported;

a) Name of the Supplier

b) Name of the Manufacturer

c) Name of importer

d) Country of Origin

e) Trade and Common names

f) Quantity to be imported

g) Registration number in case of registrable products
h) Date and invoice number

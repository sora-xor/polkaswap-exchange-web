                           A BILL ENTITLED

                 The Food and Feed Safety Act, 2017.                       Short title.


           Being an Act to provide for food and feed safety in Sierra
    Leone, to ensure the safety and quality of food and feed in order to
    protect the public against health hazards, to promote fair trade
    practices in food and feed and to provide for other related matters.

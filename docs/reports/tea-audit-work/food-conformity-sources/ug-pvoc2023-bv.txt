3. Technical requirement

Conformity requirements:
                               Compliance of goods to applicable compulsory Uganda standards shall be demonstrated
                               with test reports issued by an ISO 17025 accredited laboratories or a UNBS recognized
                               laboratory.
National deviations:                 •   Type of plugs and sockets: 3 squared pins (BS 1363). Other kind of plugs could
                                         be accepted if provided with a compliant plug adapter (male part being compliant
                                         to BS 1363)
                                     •   Voltage: products connected to the mains shall be rated for 220V or 230V or
                                         240V
                                     •   Frequency: products connected to the mains shall be rated for 50Hz (not
                                         applicable to lamps).
                                     •   Country of origin marked on the product or primary packaging
                                     •   Inspection of fishing gear: In addition to the applicable compulsory Uganda
                                         standards, the “Fish (Fishing) Rules, 2010, Statutory Instruments N0.33,
                                         particularly, Part II – Prohibited Nets and Fishing with Nets – Clause 3, 4, 6, 7
                                         and 19 shall be applied.
                                     •   Inspection of new Tyres: In addition to the applicable compulsory Uganda
                                         standards, the guideline as per 24th January 2023 shall be applied
Labelling requirements :             •   The marking shall be at least in English
                                     •   All imports with a marked limited shelf life shall have at least:
                                         - seventy five per cent (75%) remaining shelf life from the date of expected
                                         arrival in Uganda, if food products
                                         - fifty per cent (50%) remaining shelf life from the date of expected arrival in
                                         Uganda, if non-food products
Other specific requirements:   N/A

7. Facilitation procedures

•     Route B - Product Registration - for goods already registered by Bureau Veritas
•     Route C - Manufacturing Licensing - for goods already licensed by Bureau Veritas
•     Route D - for consolidated cargo imported by registered consolidators with UNBS
The following products are however not eligible for registration under Routes B and C (i.e. are subject to certification under
Route A only);
            -   Sugar
            -   Cereals and pulses such as Rice, wheat, beans, Maize etc
            -   Fertilizers
            -   Animal and Fishery products (fresh and frozen- not further processed)
            -   Fresh Dairy Products
            -   Fresh horticultural products
            -   Used or second hand good

8. Conclusion of the Assessment

      •    Certificate of Conformity (CoC)
      •    Non Conformity Report (NCR)

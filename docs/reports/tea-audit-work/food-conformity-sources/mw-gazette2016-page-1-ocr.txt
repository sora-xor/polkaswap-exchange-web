GOVERNMENT Notice No. 21

MALAWI BUREAU OF STANDARDS ACT
(CAP. 51:02f

MALAWI BUREAU OF STANDARDS (QUALITY DEVELOPMENT CESS).

( REVOCATION) ORDER, 2016

In EXERCISE of the powers conferred by section 20 of the Malawi Bureau of Standards
Act, I, JOSEPH MWANAMVEKHA, Minister of Industry, Trade and Tourism, make the

following Order—

1. This Order may be cited as the Malawi Bureau of Standards
(Quality Development Cess) (Revocation) Order, 2016.

2. A Quality Development Cess (hereinafter referred to as the
“Cess”) shall be levied, paid and collected in respect of imported
commodities specified in Column J of the Schedule at the rate specified in
Column 2 in the said First Schedule.

3. The Cess shall be payable by every person who imports
commodities specified in Column J of the First Schedule upon the entry of
the commodities into Malawi.

4. The Cess shall be payable on the listed products irrespective of
their end use or purpose.

5. The Cess shall be paid to the Malawi Bureau of Standards before
clearance of imported commodities specified in Column 1 of the First
Schedule.

6. The Cess prescribed under this Order shall be recoverable as a
debt due to the Malawi Bureau of Standards.

7.—{1) An importer of commodities specified in Column 1 in the First
Schedule shall keep records of commodities imported by him and the price
paid for such imports.

(2) An importer shall produce records referred to in subparagraph (1)
for inspection by any person designated, in writing for that purpose, by the
Director General at such time and place as that person may require.

8. Every importer of commodities specified in Column J of the First
Schedule shall furnish his name, business address and other particulars, in
Form QDCO set out in the Second Schedule, to the Director General for
registration in the register of importers kept and maintained under
paragraph 9.

9. The Director General shall keep and maintain a register of
importers of commodities specified in Column 1 in the First Schedule.

10. Any person who contravenes this Order shall, on conviction, be
liable to a fine of K250,000 and imprisonment for one year and in the case
of subsequent conviction,a fine of K500,000 and to imprisonment for two
years.

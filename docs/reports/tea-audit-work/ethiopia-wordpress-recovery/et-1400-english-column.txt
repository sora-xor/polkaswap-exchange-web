               PROCLAMATION No. 1400/2026
                PLANT PROTECTION AND
            QUARANTINE PROCLAMATION
እና      WHEREAS, it is important to enhance
ተን     production and productivity by carrying out
እና     standard plant pest surveys and surveillance,

በር     data collection, and analysis, and establishing

ዊ፣     an early warning system in collaboration with

ናዊ     relevant stakeholders for implementation of

ም      modern prevention and control technology to

ፅኦ
       reduce       the     economic,        social,       and
       environmental damage caused by pests;

ዋት      WHEREAS, it is required to establish a
ን፣     strong     and     effective     Plant     quarantine
 ት     legislative system in the country to implement
ወደ     the International Plant Protection Convention

ው      to which the country is a contracting party to

ርፍ     regulate the import and export of plants, plant

ጭ      products, and other regulated articles, restrict

ፋና     the entry and spread of pests without a serious
       negative effect on the movement of plants,
ራና
       plant products, and other regulated articles in
ኖር
       international trade; and

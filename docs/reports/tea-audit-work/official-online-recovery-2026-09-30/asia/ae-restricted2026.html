    Restricted and Prohibited Goods

    The UAE Government prohibits the import of certain goods and imposes restrictions on others. 

    Prohibited goods are those whose import and export are banned under the Common Customs Law of the GCC States, or any other law or regulation applicable in the UAE.

    Restricted goods are goods whose import and export are restricted under the same law above and applicable regulations, and they need prior approval from the authorities concerned prior to import or export.

    Prohibited Goods


    The following items are strictly prohibited from being imported or exported in the UAE:

    ·      Weed, opium, heroin, morphine, cocaine, poppy and its shells/seeds, qat, cannabis, and other narcotic substances of similar effect (all types of drugs)

    ·      Counterfeit and fake currency

    ·      Publications, photographs, paintings, drawings, cards, books, magazines, and stone sculptures that contradict Islamic teachings or public morals, or that aim to incite corruption or sedition

    ·      Three-layer nylon fishing nets

    ·      Gambling tools and machines

    ·      Goods imported from boycotted countries

    ·      Ozone-depleting substances

    ·      Radioactive-contaminated materials

    ·      Asbestos sheets and pipes

    ·      Laser pens emitting red light beams

    ·      Hazardous waste

    ·      Naswar (a form of smokeless tobacco)

    ·      Betel leaves

    ·      Used or refurbished tyres

     

     

     


    Identify Your Goods with the Smart HS Classification Tool:


     

    To ensure compliance and determine the correct Harmonised System (HS) code for your goods, including any applicable customs duties, taxes, or restrictions, use the Smart HS Classification Tool.

    This tool is a specialised search engine developed by Abu Dhabi Customs to help you quickly find the correct Harmonised System (HS) code for your import or export commodities. It also indicates any associated approvals or regulatory requirements.

    Access the Smart HS Classification Tool on TAMM

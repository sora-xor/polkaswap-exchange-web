     In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2) of the Plant Protection Act, 2023, the
     Cabinet makes the following Order —


1.   Citation
     1.   This Order may be cited as the Plant Protection Act, 2023 (Commencement) Order,
          2026.
2.   Commencement
     2.   The Plant Protection Act, 2023 comes into force on 1st November, 2026.

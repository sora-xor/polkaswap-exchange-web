Travel
When Traveling can I bring in meat and food items in my luggage?
Individual traveling passengers are allowed to import eligible meat and meat products from eligible countries, intended for personal consumption up to a maximum of 5 kg (11 lbs.), without an import permit or sanitary certificate provided the original packaging is intact and the packaging bears the inspection mark/icon from the competent government authority in the country of export.
Cooked or frozen seafood of up to 20 lbs. is allowed for personal consumption, providing that there is proof of purchase and/or inspection.
Game meats from eligible countries would require an import permit from the Cayman Islands Department of Agriculture regardless of the quantum or processing.
Fresh fruits and vegetables would require an import permit from the Cayman Islands Department of Agriculture regardless of the quantum.
You are allowed to travel with other cooked /processed foods products for your personal consumption, the attached brochure will also provide more information.
You can also let us know the country that you will be traveling from so that we can provide specific guidance.
Please feel free to contact
AHIS on 1-345-946-2967 / 1-345-949-7909 or email ciagricultureimports@gov.ky
if you need more information.
When Traveling What Items MUST be Declared with Customs and Border Control?
Bringing back Agricultural Products in Luggage
Bringing back Processed Food in Luggage – Commercially processed, canned, cooked, shelf-stable foods are allowed.
Home-canned products are not allowed entry
because canning practices can vary and may not remove all pest and disease risks.
All listed products MUST be declared to Customs and Border Control for an Agricultural Inspection on arrival to determine pest presence and any other Regulations might be in place for the products.

Import/Export
Can I purchase seeds online and Import them for planting?
Yes, all seeds for propagation regardless of the quantity require a valid Import Permit. Certified and sealed commercially packaged seeds from authorized seed companies are allowed without a phytosanitary certificate from the country of export. Loose seeds, weed species, invasive and exotic species are further regulated.
Email: ciagricultureimports@gov.ky for the permit application.

Can I import dairy milk and dairy milk products?
Importation of Dairy Milk and Dairy Milk Products in Passenger Baggage for Personal Use
Individual traveling passengers are allowed to import a small quantity of unpasteurized dairy milk products (less than 0.5 lbs. of soft cheeses and less than 1.0 lbs. of hard cheeses) for personal consumption only. These small quantities of unpasteurised dairy milk products are prohibited for resale to the general public.
Pasteurised dairy milk and cheese products that are less than the limit for personal consignment may be imported without an import licence provided the original packaging is intact and the packaging bears the inspection mark/icon from the competent government authority.

Can I import Cabonba Caroliniana into the Cayman Islands?
High risk and NOT recommended for import:
Cabomba Carolina
This species is a well-documented invasive weed globally.
Cabomba carolina
forms dense uniform beds of growth in freshwater bodies it colonizes. It outcompetes native flora and negatively impacts animal communities within those habitats. It has limited tolerance to salinity and fresh water habitats are limited within the Cayman Islands, but this species poses an high risk threat to them if it were to escape its intended use. This species can reproduce vegatatively, even from discarded small plant fragments making escape into the wild likely.

What does the Agricultural Health Inspection Services (AHIS) do?
Protecting the flora and fauna of the Cayman Islands from the introduction and establishment of pests and diseases through a strong National Sanitary and Phytosanitary (SPS) Regime.
Promote the safe use, handling and storage of pesticides and of the regulation of importation and distribution of these products.

Importation
How to export your pets to the UK/EU?
All dogs/cats must have microchips and current rabies vaccination to be eligible to travel to the UK/ EU. There is also a 21-day waiting period after the most recent rabies vaccination before your pet(s) would be eligible to enter the EU. You must complete the Rabies Vaccination information sheet and send it to
DOARabiesVaccines@gov.ky
so that an appointment will be booked for your pet(s).
Rabies vaccination costs CI$25/pet and is payable in cash, CI cheque or debit cards.
EU and UK export health certificates for dogs/cats are valid for 5 days (120 hours to be exact) from the date and time of the tapeworm treatment at your local Vet Clinic. Example: The health certificate issued on 16
th
August is valid for EU entry until 21
st
August.
Once you have booked an export appointment at a local Vet Clinic, you must book an appointment with Department of Agriculture to endorse the EU health certificate by calling DoA at (345) 947-3090 or email us on
ci.agricultureVS@gov.ky
. DoA do not need to see your pet(s) during the endorsement appointment, just the original EU health certificate along with copies of your pets’ proof of microchip implantation date and current rabies vaccination certificates.
If your pet(s) are traveling in- transit via USA, the Cayman Islands local Vet Clinic will email DoA the North American health certificates for endorsement (after the examination).
Export permit $20 KYD per pet and the endorsement fee $20 KYD.
Do Cruise ship travelers with pets need an import permit?
Cruise Ship Travelers must apply for a single entry import permit in order for their dog or cat to disembark the cruise ship and set foot on Cayman Islands soil.

Is the titre test mandatory for importing pets into the Cayman Islands?
Yes, a current titre test is mandatory for all dogs and cats entering the Cayman Islands, regardless of traveling via cruise ship or airplane, or the rabies-risk status of their country of origin or export.
Note that Section I of the import requirements states that service and support animals must meet the same entry requirements as regular pets
Application – Conditions Governing the Importation of dogs and cats into the Cayman Islands click
here
or visit the forms section on the import and export services page.
What happens if my pet fails the rabies antibody (titer) test required for importation into the Cayman Islands?
If your pet does not meet the required antibody level on the rabies titer test, it must be
re-vaccinated against rabies,
and you must
wait at least 30 days
before collecting a new blood sample for another titer test. The import approval process can only proceed once the required level test results are received.

Pet Passport
What are the requirements to get a pet passport?
Your pet(s) must complete three round trip travels (single entry import permit and export permit) without running into any issues before being eligible for a pet passport.
Please refer to section F of our importation requirements for more details on pet passports.
email: ci.agricultureVS@gov.ky and the auto reply will have the application form to complete and send back.
As a pet passport holder what do I submit for the Import Permit?
As a pet passport holder, you are still required to submit the following documents to email: ci.agricultureVS@gov.ky to be reviewed and approved.
Your email to ci.agricultureVS@gov.ky should contain the following information:
Copy of the Titre results
Rabies Vaccination results / certificate
Health Certificate from local veterinary clinic (please travel with the original document)
Reference the Pet Passport Number in the email
Provide the flight details (Airline, time, date, etc)
What are the payment options for pet passport fees?
For local payment, you can pay the pet passport fee (CI$150.00/pet) in cash, credit / debit card or Cayman Islands cheque, upon collection.

Abattoir
What is the Department of Agriculture Abattoir?
The Cayman Islands Department of Agriculture provides an abattoir facility and services for the slaughter and dressing of livestock fit for human consumption in compliance with relevant national legislation, policies, procedures, and international standards. Currently, the abattoir services cattle, goat, and pig farmers to assist with the provision of wholesome beef, chevon (goat meat), and pork for Cayman Islands residents and tourists.
How are the animals slaughtered at the DoA Abattoir?
The Cayman Islands Department of Agriculture provides an abattoir facility and services for the slaughter and dressing of livestock fit for human consumption in compliance with relevant national legislation, policies, procedures, and international standards.
The animals at the abattoir facility are stunned prior to bleeding-out and slaughtered.
How do I arrange for slaughter services from the Abattoir?
To arrange for slaughter services, persons should call 345-926-2212/ 345-947-3090. Alternatively, bookings can be made through the Department’s website, by completing the schedule a service form at the bottom of the page.
Abattoir clients should provide the following information when arranging slaughter services:
Name
Telephone number
Email address
Type and number of livestock to be slaughtered
Preferred date for delivery of carcasses/meat
Preferred delivery location
For more information about the Department’s abattoir, please contact the Senior Veterinary Officer (Acting) on 345-947-3090 / 345-925-3920 or
samantha.dorman@gov.ky

      Sint Maarten Veterinary Clinics
      		​​​


      Unit LVV is a sub-section of Section Economic Control charged with the responsibility of monitoring the importation and exportation of small (-domesticated) animals, and the importation of plants and plant produce; to safeguard the local flora and fauna on Sint Maarten from contamination and invasion of foreign plants and animals that would disturb our ecosystem.  On this page, you can find the conditions and application forms for import and export of small (domesticated) animals.

Click on the “Get Started” button to complete the application form.

Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us via email at quarantine@agriculture.gov.bb.

Step One

Start the Application Form

  • This form is divided into four (4) sections
  • Please follow the instructions in each section until the application form is completed
  • All questions are mandatory
  • You are not required to complete the application form in one sitting
  • You may use the “Save Application” button and return to the application form later

Submit Application Form

Tips
  • Attach any relevant information that can assist in the processing of this Import Permit application.
  • Applications will be processed within five (5) working days (under normal conditions) from date of receipt.
  • Due to the danger of introducing Viral, Fungal and Bacterial diseases, Import Permits will NOT be issued for the importation of Citrus, Avocado or Mango plants.
  • The original Import Permit Application must be submitted before the Import Permit is issued.
  • INCOMPLETE and/or ILLEGIBLE APPLICATIONS WILL NOT BE PROCESSED.

Tips

It is an offense to knowingly or recklessly provide information which is false for the purpose of obtaining any document under the Plant Protection Act – 2007 (53). It is also an offence to alter, forge, deface or destroy any document issued under this Act. Such offenses may result in a fine of $1,500.00, imprisonment for a term of one year, or both, and the confiscation and destruction of the commodity in accordance with the Plant Protection Act – 2007 (53).

On acceptance of the Import Permit and receipt of the items, the Importer accepts that, on inspection, if any plant pests are present on or within the consignment, the items will be treated, re-exported or confiscated and destroyed at the importer’s expense. If treatment of the items is necessary, the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security accepts no responsibility for any damage which may occur to these items.

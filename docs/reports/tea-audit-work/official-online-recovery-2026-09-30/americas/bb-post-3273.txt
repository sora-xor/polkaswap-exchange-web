
 S.I. 2024 No. 22 



 By Her Excellency The Most Honourable Dame Sandra Prunella Mason, Freedom of Barbados, Dame Grand Cross of the Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Dame of Saint Andrew, Senior Counsel, Doctor of Laws, President of Barbados. 



 S. MASON President 



 A PROCLAMATION 



  WHEREAS  by section 83 of the   Animal Health and Veterinary Public Health       Act, 2023  (Act 2023-1)  it is provided that the Act shall come into operation on a date to be fixed by Proclamation; 



  AND WHEREAS  it is expedient that the Act comes into operation on the 29th  day of May, 2024; 



  NOW THEREFORE  by virtue of the power and authority in me vested, I hereby appoint the 29th day of May, 2024 as the day on which the Act shall come into operation. 



 Given under my hand and the Public Seal of Barbados this  28th day of May, 2024.

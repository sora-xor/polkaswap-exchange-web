This thematic area categorization list is prepared based on the the list of Eritrean Laws previously
prepared by the project under title ‘Complete List of Domestic Legislations of the State of Eritrea’.
Unlike the previous document, this categorization list only provides for the English version of the
title of the legislation, and only if there is no English version is the Tigrinya version used. However,
the available language versions are provided in a separate column of the table.

For additional brief information on the legislations, refer the ‘Complete List of Domestic
Legislations of the State of Eritrea’.

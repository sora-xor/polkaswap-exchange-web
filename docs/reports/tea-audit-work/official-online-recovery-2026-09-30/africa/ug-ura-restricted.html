      Restricted Goods

      Restricted goods are goods whose importation or exportation into Uganda is regulated and requires prior clearance by the competent government agency. Restricted/Controlled goods may be imported or exported provided the importer or exporter has the necessary authorization/permits. For example, foodstuffs will require the proper permits from Health and or Agricultural Authorities, plants and plant products require clearance from the Ministry of Agro-Industry and Food Security, while pharmaceutical products require clearance from the Government Pharmacist.

      NOTE: In case the authorities refuse to approve the application for a permit due to various reasons such as health, phytosanitary or security hazards, the imported goods would be liable to seizure.

      Restricted goods include the following;
      • All goods the exportation of which is regulated under this Act or any law for the time being in force in the partner state.
      • Timber from any wood grown in the partner states
      • Fresh unprocessed fish (Nile Perch and Tilapia)
      • Wood charcoal
      • Drone cameras
      • The export of any minerals such as gold

      Restricted exports

      1

      Postal franking machines except and in accordance with the terms of a written permit granted by a competent authority of the Partner State.

      The following goods shall not be exported in vessels of less than two hundred and fifty tons register—

      ·       Warehoused goods;

      ·       Goods under duty drawback;

      ·       Transhipped goods.

      2.

      Traps capable of killing or capturing any game animal except and in accordance with the terms of a written permit granted by the Partner State.

      1. Rice

      2. Wheat or Meslin flour

      3. Sand

      4. Limestone

      5. Cement

      6. Textiles and textile articles for export to the USA and Canada

      7. Rough diamonds
